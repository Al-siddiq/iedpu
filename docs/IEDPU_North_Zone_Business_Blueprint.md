# DOCUMENT CONTROL

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document establishes the vision, objectives, operational principles, functional expectations, administrative structure and long-term direction of the proposed IEDPU North Zone Digital Membership, Administration and Development Portal.

It serves as the common reference document for:

1. IEDPU leadership and relevant approving authorities.
2. The North Zone Initiative Committee.
3. Branch Chairmen and Branch Executive Committees.
4. Members of IEDPU.
5. The appointed software development team.
6. Future technical and administrative personnel responsible for the platform.

The document defines what the organisation intends to achieve through the portal and how the proposed platform should operate.

It is deliberately non-technical. It does not prescribe programming languages, database structures, software frameworks, API specifications or other implementation details. Those will be addressed in a separate technical blueprint developed from this document.

**Every subsequent technical specification and development decision should remain consistent with the objectives and operational requirements established here.**

# PART ONE: PROJECT FOUNDATION

## 2. Background and Rationale

The Ilorin Emirate Descendants Progressive Union (IEDPU) is an organisation established to promote unity, progress, cooperation, socioeconomic development, education, welfare and the collective interests of Ilorin Emirate descendants.

Over time, the organisation has established branches across different geographical locations, including the Northern Zone.

However, managing a geographically distributed organisation creates administrative challenges, particularly in maintaining accurate membership information, coordinating branches, managing financial contributions, communicating official information and monitoring organisational activities.

Membership information may exist in physical registers, spreadsheets, personal records, branch documents and informal communication channels.

This makes it difficult to establish a consolidated and dependable understanding of the organisation's actual membership strength.

The proposed digital portal is intended to address these challenges by creating a centralised, secure and professionally managed digital environment for IEDPU North Zone.

The initial priority is to digitally identify, register, verify and organise existing IEDPU members across the 19 Northern States and the Federal Capital Territory.

Beyond membership registration, the platform will gradually become a comprehensive administrative tool supporting members, branches, zonal administration and the Initiative Committee.

The ultimate objective is to strengthen organisational administration through reliable information, transparent financial records, accountable decision-making and effective communication.

## 3. Project Vision

To establish a comprehensive, secure, accessible and sustainable digital platform that strengthens IEDPU's membership management, administrative efficiency, financial accountability, communication and organisational development across Northern Nigeria.

## 4. Project Mission

To provide a unified digital environment through which existing IEDPU members can establish and maintain their digital membership records, access organisational services, fulfil approved financial obligations, obtain membership identification and participate more effectively in the affairs of the Union.

The platform will also equip authorised IEDPU administrators with appropriate tools to coordinate activities, manage membership information, communicate with members and monitor organisational performance.

## 5. Project Objectives

The project seeks to:

1. Establish a comprehensive digital register of existing IEDPU North Zone members.
2. Identify the actual membership strength of participating branches.
3. Establish reliable membership records through branch-based verification.
4. Provide members with secure individual accounts and digital profiles.
5. Introduce a professional and verifiable digital membership identification system.
6. Improve the management, collection, tracking and reconciliation of membership dues and other approved financial obligations.
7. Provide members with complete and transparent financial transaction histories.
8. Strengthen communication between the Initiative Committee, zonal administration, branches and individual members.
9. Provide flexible administrative tools for authorised IEDPU officials and project stakeholders.
10. Establish comprehensive accountability and auditing of administrative and financial activities.
11. Make the IEDPU Constitution and other approved organisational documents accessible through the portal.
12. Provide reliable membership statistics and administrative reports.
13. Establish a foundation for additional administrative, financial, welfare and development services.
14. Prepare the organisation for a future population census covering eligible Ilorin Emirate descendants who are not currently IEDPU members.

# PART TWO: PROJECT SCOPE

## 6. Initial Population and Geographical Coverage

### 6.1 Target Population

The initial registration exercise shall be restricted to existing IEDPU members.

The project will not initially register the entire population of Ilorin Emirate descendants residing in Northern Nigeria.

This decision is based on the principle that IEDPU should first establish an accurate understanding of its existing membership strength before expanding the exercise to the wider population.

### 6.2 Geographical Coverage

The portal shall cover:

- The 19 Northern States of Nigeria.
- The Federal Capital Territory (FCT).

### 6.3 Individual Registration

Registration shall be individual-based.

Every member shall have a separate membership record and, where applicable, an individual login account.

Household registration will not form part of the initial implementation.

### 6.4 Future Expansion

Following the successful establishment of the membership platform, IEDPU may expand the system to accommodate:

- Ilorin Emirate descendants who are not existing IEDPU members.
- Wider community population registration.
- Additional geographical areas.
- New branches approved by the appropriate IEDPU authorities.
- Additional organisational and community development services.

The initial architecture should accommodate this expansion without requiring the organisation to rebuild the entire platform.

## 7. Project Scope and Functional Areas

The portal shall progressively accommodate the following major functional areas:

1. Public information and organisational website.
2. Member registration and membership verification.
3. Member accounts and personal profiles.
4. Branch and zonal administration.
5. Flexible user roles and permission management.
6. Financial obligations and dues management.
7. Member financial statements and payment history.
8. Digital membership identification.
9. Organisational announcements and communication.
10. Constitutional and document repository.
11. Comprehensive administrative audit and accountability.
12. Management dashboards and reporting.
13. Future administrative and organisational development modules.

The first implementation will prioritise the foundation necessary to support these areas, while additional services will be introduced progressively.

# PART THREE: GOVERNANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

## 8. Administrative Governance Structure

The portal shall operate under the authority of the appropriate IEDPU structures.

For the purposes of administering this particular project, the Initiative Committee shall serve as the highest operational administrative authority within the platform, subject to the authority and approvals delegated by the appropriate constitutional structures.

The proposed operational hierarchy is:

1. IEDPU North Zone Initiative Committee.
2. Branch Chairmen.
3. Authorised Branch Executive Members.
4. Enumerators.
5. Registered Members.

The development team shall have a separate technical administration interface for maintaining and managing the platform.

The developer's technical responsibilities shall not automatically confer organisational or constitutional authority.

## 9. Separation of Official IEDPU Offices and Portal Roles

This is a fundamental principle of the proposed system.

**An official position held within IEDPU shall be distinct from the administrative role assigned to an individual within the digital portal.**

The portal must maintain these as two separate records.

### 9.1 Official IEDPU Office

This describes the individual's recognised organisational position.

Examples include:

- Zonal Chairman.
- Zonal Secretary.
- Branch Chairman.
- Branch Secretary.
- Branch Financial Secretary.
- Branch Treasurer.
- Other recognised Branch Executive positions.

The official position establishes the individual's organisational identity and affiliation.

### 9.2 Portal Administrative Role

This describes the specific activities the individual is authorised to perform within the software.

Examples include:

- Membership Verification Officer.
- Branch Registration Administrator.
- Financial Records Officer.
- Announcement Administrator.
- Branch Reporting Officer.
- Member Support Administrator.
- Branch Due Management Officer.

An individual may hold an official IEDPU office without receiving administrative access to the portal.

Similarly, an individual may receive a portal administrative role without holding the corresponding official IEDPU office.

For example, an individual recognised as a Branch Chairman may be assigned the portal role of Membership Verification Officer. Another individual without an elected branch position may be authorised as a Branch Registration Administrator.

The system must never assume that an official title automatically grants unrestricted software permissions.

### 9.3 Flexible Role Assignment

The Initiative Committee shall have the authority, within its approved mandate, to assign predefined portal roles to individuals.

The Committee should be able to:

- Create authorised stakeholder accounts.
- Assign portal roles.
- Change assigned roles.
- Assign multiple roles where necessary.
- Restrict roles to specific branches or geographical areas.
- Set the effective period of an assignment.
- Suspend or revoke access.
- Review the responsibilities attached to each role.

Portal role profiles shall be created, configured, deactivated and assigned by authorised administrators through the portal, using only the predefined, release-controlled catalogue of executable permissions. No role name or role ID shall be hardcoded into business logic.

The Committee should not need to request software redevelopment whenever it wants to assign an existing administrative responsibility to another person.

### 9.4 Permission-Based Administration

Each portal role shall have clearly defined permissions.

For example, the Membership Verification Officer may review and approve pending membership records but should not automatically have permission to change financial obligations or assign super-administrator privileges.

The Financial Records Officer may view authorised financial information and manage assigned payment records but should not automatically be able to modify membership verification decisions.

This approach will provide flexibility without sacrificing accountability.

## 9.5 Engineering clarification — administrator-managed organisational data

**Project commencement:** The project owner confirms the project is commissioned and development may commence. P0 reconciles engineering decisions; it is not a second permission-to-develop gate. Feature-specific constitutional, privacy and financial activation restrictions still apply.

**Organisation:** Authorised Committee administrators manage zone reference records, approved project coverage, branches, office types, Branch Chairmen and other office holders through protected interfaces. No branch name, zone name, Chairman identity or portal role profile is compiled as immutable business data. Northern Zone remains the approved operational scope; reference data for other zones does not extend that mandate.

**Branches:** The historic constitutional branch schedule is optional reference material, not a mandatory import or automatically active directory. New branches default to pending recognition and inactive. The **Committee Super Administrator may activate a branch directly** after recognition/location validation, with reason/reference and an audit event; no second approver is required for routine branch activation.

**Office holders:** An office-holder name and term may be recorded before a canonical member or account exists. Such an unlinked record is informational and cannot authenticate or verify members. Later account/member linking and separately approved scoped portal roles are required for executable access.

**Roles:** Administrators may create, update, deactivate and assign role profiles and permission bundles from the developer-maintained executable permission catalogue, within protected grant ceilings. They cannot invent backend permission keys, self-elevate or derive portal privileges from an official title.

**Separation of duties:** The Committee Super Administrator handles operational records and approvals. A separate developer interface handles deployments, configuration, backups, migrations, security diagnostics and specialist technical work. Developers are IEDPU members and may also receive independently authorised organisational roles, but technical access does not confer Committee authority.

**Registration/privacy approval:** The **Committee Super Administrator** approves versioned registration rules and privacy notices before live use. Approval does not displace lawful-basis, controller, retention or other privacy obligations.

**Activation holds:** Final registration fields, live personal-data processing, official member numbers, payment providers, official digital IDs and external messaging remain gated by their relevant authorities. These holds do not block unrelated P1 coding.

See `docs/implementation/P0/Engineering_Decision_Register.md` for binding decisions and activation holds.

# PART FOUR: MEMBERSHIP MANAGEMENT

## 10. Member Registration

The portal shall provide a structured registration process for existing IEDPU members.

Registration shall be available through:

- Independent online registration.
- Assisted registration by authorised personnel.
- Registration by trained enumerators.

The registration process shall capture the information approved for membership identification and organisational planning.

The information may include:

- Full name.
- Gender.
- Date of birth or approved age information.
- Photograph.
- Telephone number.
- Email address, where available.
- Ancestral information.
- State of residence.
- Local Government Area.
- Town or community of residence.
- Existing IEDPU branch.
- Official IEDPU position, where applicable.
- Other approved membership information.

The portal shall issue a unique membership registration reference.

Registration alone shall not automatically establish verified membership.

## 11. Membership Verification

Verification shall primarily be undertaken at the branch level.

This approach recognises that Branch Chairmen and their Executive Committees are best positioned to identify and confirm their existing members.

### Proposed verification process

1. A member submits a registration.
2. The portal identifies the selected branch.
3. The registration appears in the branch's verification queue.
4. The Branch Chairman or an authorised Branch Executive reviews the submission.
5. The officer confirms the individual's existing membership or requests additional clarification.
6. The decision is recorded in the portal.
7. Unresolved or disputed cases are referred to the appropriate administrative authority.
8. Successful verification activates the applicable member services.

The Branch Chairman shall have final branch-level verification responsibility, with delegated preliminary review available to authorised Branch Executives.

The Initiative Committee shall supervise the overall process and address escalated cases within its authority.

### Membership statuses

The portal should distinguish:

- Registration initiated.
- Registration submitted.
- Pending verification.
- Information correction required.
- Verified member.
- Verification disputed.
- Suspended membership, where properly authorised.
- Former or transferred member, where applicable.

Financial standing must be maintained separately from membership verification.

A genuine member who has outstanding dues should not automatically be classified as an unverified person.

## 12. Member Accounts and Personal Profiles

Every registered member should have access to an individual member account.

The member dashboard should provide access to:

- Personal profile.
- Membership details.
- Branch information.
- Membership identification.
- Financial obligations.
- Payment history.
- Official announcements.
- Organisational documents.
- Support and correction requests.
- Account settings.

Members should be able to update permitted personal information.

Changes affecting verified identity, branch affiliation or other important membership information should pass through the appropriate review procedure.

The platform should also provide secure account recovery and protection against unauthorised access.

# PART FIVE: MEMBERSHIP IDENTIFICATION

## 13. Professional Digital Membership ID

The portal shall introduce a simple, professional and consistent IEDPU membership identification system.

Every verified member eligible for official identification shall receive a unique membership number.

A proposed numbering format is:

**IEDPU-NZ-000001**

The number shall be unique and permanently associated with the member's record.

The identification number should not depend on the member's branch or current location because members may relocate or transfer between branches.

### 13.1 Digital Membership Card

The proposed membership card shall contain:

- IEDPU name and approved emblem.
- Member photograph.
- Full name.
- Unique membership identification number.
- North Zone identification.
- Current branch.
- Date of issuance.
- QR code for verification.

### 13.2 QR Verification

The QR code shall allow authorised persons to confirm that a membership card is genuine.

The verification page shall reveal only the minimum information necessary for confirmation.

It must not expose sensitive personal information such as telephone numbers, home addresses or complete member profiles.

### 13.3 Card Management

The system should support:

- Card generation.
- Card reissuance.
- Replacement of lost cards.
- Card suspension where properly authorised.
- Verification of card authenticity.
- Recording of card issuance history.

Official issuance must be subject to the appropriate IEDPU constitutional authorisation or delegation, particularly the provision concerning membership identity cards under Section 4 of the Constitution.

# PART SIX: FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

## 14. Flexible Financial Obligations and Dues Management

The financial management system shall not rely on permanently hardcoded dues categories or amounts.

The Super Administrator shall have the ability to create and manage financial obligations as organisational needs arise, within the applicable approval structure.

The system should support both recurring and one-time financial obligations.

### 14.1 Creation of Financial Obligations

An authorised administrator should be able to create a new financial obligation by specifying:

- Name or title of the obligation.
- Description and reason for the payment.
- Amount payable.
- Currency.
- Target recipients.
- Date of commencement.
- Payment deadline.
- Applicable payment destination.
- Whether the obligation is recurring or one-time.
- Any additional approved payment instructions.

The administrator should be able to determine who is required to pay.

### 14.2 Targeting Options

A financial obligation may be assigned to:

**A. Every eligible member**

For example, an approved general contribution applicable to all verified members.

**B. An individual member**

For example, an approved individual financial obligation.

**C. A particular branch**

For example, a contribution applicable to all members of Abuja Branch.

**D. Selected branches**

For example, a contribution applicable to three designated branches.

**E. A defined group of individuals**

For example, an approved obligation applicable to selected members or a particular group of authorised stakeholders.

**F. Members selected through approved criteria**

For example, members belonging to a particular branch or assigned administrative group.

The system should support targeted financial obligations without requiring developers to modify the software whenever a new payment category is introduced.

### 14.3 Payment Deadlines

Each obligation shall have an identifiable payment deadline.

The system should support:

- Due date.
- Due time, where necessary.
- Recurrence period, where applicable.
- Payment status.
- Outstanding balance.
- Approved extensions.
- Waivers and adjustments, where authorised.

The portal should automatically identify obligations that are pending, approaching their deadline, overdue or fully paid.

No financial penalty should be imposed automatically unless an appropriate authority has approved the applicable rule.

### 14.4 Financial Obligation Management

Authorised administrators should be able to:

- Create obligations.
- View assigned recipients.
- Monitor payments.
- Extend deadlines.
- Issue approved reminders.
- Suspend future collection.
- Cancel an obligation where authorised.
- Record approved waivers.
- Review collection performance.

Once an obligation has been assigned and payments have started, changes should not silently rewrite existing financial history.

Any correction must be properly recorded and auditable.

## 15. Branch-Based Due Automation

IEDPU branches should be able to request the automation of their branch financial obligations through the portal.

This will allow individual branches to manage their approved dues within the central platform.

### 15.1 Branch Request

An authorised Branch Chairman or designated Branch Executive should be able to submit a request containing:

- Branch name.
- Description of the proposed dues.
- Reason for the dues.
- Proposed amount.
- Applicable members.
- Payment frequency.
- Commencement date.
- Payment deadline.
- Proposed receiving bank details.
- Any supporting approval or documentation.

### 15.2 Approval Process

A branch request shall not automatically become an active payment obligation.

The request should be submitted for review by the authorised Initiative Committee administrator or other designated approving authority.

The Committee should be able to:

- Approve the request.
- Reject the request with a reason.
- Request clarification.
- Return it for modification.

Once approved, the obligation can be activated for the designated branch members.

### 15.3 Branch Payment Destination

The portal shall accommodate approved branch payment destinations.

The submitted bank information should be verified before being activated.

Changes to approved receiving accounts should require additional verification and authorisation.

The platform should maintain a history of:

- Submitted bank details.
- Account verification.
- Approval decisions.
- Changes to account information.
- Dates of activation and deactivation.

Sensitive bank information must not be publicly accessible.

### 15.4 Financial Transparency

The platform should clearly distinguish between:

- Zonal obligations.
- Branch obligations.
- Individual obligations.
- General organisational contributions.

The system should also distinguish the amount paid by members from the amount successfully settled into the designated bank account.

The platform is intended to provide financial administration and accountability. It should not be treated as a replacement for the Union's approved banking arrangements or statutory financial records.

# PART SEVEN: MEMBER FINANCIAL STATEMENT

## 16. Comprehensive Member Financial History

One of the most important financial features shall be an individual member financial statement.

Every member should be able to access a complete and chronological record of all financial transactions recorded against their membership account.

The presentation should resemble a professional bank statement.

### 16.1 Statement Information

The statement should show:

- Transaction date.
- Transaction reference.
- Payment description.
- Financial obligation.
- Amount charged.
- Amount paid.
- Outstanding balance.
- Payment channel.
- Payment status.
- Receiving branch or organisational destination.
- Receipt reference.

### 16.2 Transaction Categories

The statement should distinguish:

- Successful payments.
- Pending payments.
- Failed payments.
- Reversed transactions.
- Approved refunds.
- Financial adjustments.
- Waivers.
- Outstanding obligations.

### 16.3 Financial Accountability

The system must preserve the history of every financial transaction.

No authorised administrator should be able to silently delete or rewrite a completed payment record.

Where a mistake occurs, the correction must be recorded as a properly authorised adjustment, reversal or refund.

The original transaction must remain visible in the historical record.

### 16.4 Member Financial Statement

Members should be able to:

- View their complete payment history.
- View current outstanding obligations.
- Download or print their statements.
- Retrieve individual payment receipts.
- Identify overdue payments.
- Track pending transactions.
- Raise a financial complaint or reconciliation request.

The financial statement should provide a reliable account of the member's recorded financial relationship with IEDPU.

# PART ELEVEN: AUDIT, ACCOUNTABILITY AND TRANSPARENCY

## 20. Comprehensive Administrative Audit System

A rigorous and comprehensive audit system shall be one of the foundational pillars of the IEDPU portal.

The system must maintain a reliable record of important activities performed by every authorised user.

This requirement applies to the Initiative Committee, administrators, Branch Chairmen, Branch Executives, Enumerators, members and technical personnel according to their respective access privileges.

**No individual should be able to perform a sensitive administrative or financial action without the action being recorded and traceable.**

### 20.1 Actions to Be Audited

The audit system should capture, among other things:

**User and access activities**
- Account creation.
- Login and logout.
- Failed login attempts.
- Password and account recovery.
- Account suspension and reactivation.
- Changes to access privileges.
- Role assignments and removals.
- Changes to administrative permissions.

**Membership activities**
- New member registration.
- Profile creation.
- Changes to member information.
- Branch transfers.
- Verification decisions.
- Rejected applications.
- Membership suspension.
- Account linking and recovery.
- Member record exports.

**Financial activities**
- Creation of dues.
- Changes to financial obligations.
- Assignment of dues to recipients.
- Payment initiation.
- Payment confirmation.
- Failed transactions.
- Refunds.
- Reversals.
- Waivers.
- Financial adjustments.
- Changes to payment destinations.
- Manual payment recording.
- Financial statement generation and export.

**Communication activities**
- Announcement creation.
- Editing.
- Publication.
- Scheduling.
- Cancellation.
- Recipient selection.
- Bulk communication.
- Announcement deletion or archival.

**Administrative activities**
- Branch creation and modification.
- Changes to organisational records.
- Creation of stakeholder accounts.
- Changes to official office information.
- Administrative approvals.
- Data exports.
- Configuration changes.

**Technical and security activities**
- Changes to system settings.
- Changes to security configuration.
- Access to protected administrative functions.
- Database maintenance operations.
- Backup and restoration activities.
- Privileged technical access.

### 20.2 Audit Record Information

Every important recorded action should capture:

- Identity of the person performing the action.
- Assigned portal role at the time.
- Date and time.
- Type of action.
- Record or information affected.
- Previous value, where applicable.
- New value, where applicable.
- Reason for the action, particularly for sensitive operations.
- Source of the action.
- Relevant device, session and network information where available.
- Success or failure status.

### 20.3 Protection of Audit Records

Audit records must be protected from unauthorised alteration or deletion.

Ordinary administrators should not be able to erase their activity history.

The system should maintain a separate audit access permission, so that only appropriately authorised personnel can review sensitive audit information.

Access to audit records should itself be recorded.

### 20.4 High-Risk Actions

Certain activities should require stronger controls.

These include:

- Granting Super Administrator privileges.
- Changing financial account details.
- Approving major financial adjustments.
- Reversing completed transactions.
- Bulk exporting member information.
- Changing verification decisions.
- Altering sensitive organisational records.
- Changing critical system settings.

For particularly sensitive operations, the portal should support additional confirmation, reason recording and approval by a second authorised person.

### 20.5 Audit Monitoring and Reporting

The Committee should have access to an audit dashboard that can identify:

- Who performed an action.
- What was changed.
- When it happened.
- Which record was affected.
- Whether the action was authorised.
- Whether the action succeeded.
- Whether unusual activity occurred.

The system should also be capable of flagging suspicious patterns for review.

Audit reports should be exportable by appropriately authorised personnel.

### 20.6 Audit Independence

The ability to administer the portal must not automatically confer the ability to erase audit history.

The developer should not be able to secretly modify production audit records.

The organisation should retain control over the audit retention policy and access to its records.

The system should preserve audit records through personnel changes, administrative restructuring and future software upgrades.

# PART TWELVE: DATA GOVERNANCE AND OWNERSHIP

## 21. Data Ownership and Responsibility

All membership and organisational information collected through the portal shall be held for the authorised purposes of IEDPU.

The appropriate IEDPU legal entity shall be identified as the responsible data controller, subject to confirmation of its legal and governance arrangements.

The Initiative Committee shall serve as the operational custodian of the project data under its delegated mandate.

Branch officers shall be responsible for the accuracy of membership information within their assigned operational responsibilities.

The development team shall provide technical services and process data only within its authorised mandate.

### 21.1 Data Protection Principles

The platform shall:

- Collect information for defined organisational purposes.
- Restrict access according to assigned responsibilities.
- Protect personal information from unauthorised disclosure.
- Maintain appropriate consent and privacy records.
- Allow members to request corrections.
- Establish appropriate data retention procedures.
- Protect information during transmission and storage.
- Maintain secure backups.
- Provide procedures for responding to data breaches.
- Comply with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements.

### 21.2 Member Information

Members should have appropriate access to their own information and the ability to request corrections.

They should not have access to another member's confidential information without an approved reason and authority.

Publicly available membership verification should reveal only limited information.

# PART THIRTEEN: WEBSITE, DOMAIN AND HOSTING

## 22. Official Domain

The proposed official domain shall be:

**iedpu.ng**

The domain will serve as the primary online identity of the IEDPU North Zone digital platform, subject to domain availability and registration approval.

The website should present a professional organisational identity and provide a reliable entry point to the various portal services.

### 22.1 Domain Ownership

The domain should be registered and controlled on behalf of IEDPU.

It should not be registered exclusively under the personal ownership of an individual developer.

The Committee should maintain appropriate administrative access and renewal oversight.

## 23. Hosting Arrangement

The initial hosting arrangement shall use a suitable shared hosting server, as proposed by the project leadership.

The selected hosting provider must support the operational and security requirements of the portal.

Before production deployment, the development team should confirm that the shared hosting environment can adequately support:

- Secure member accounts.
- The required application environment.
- Database protection.
- Secure backups.
- Automated tasks and notifications.
- Payment integrations.
- Appropriate access restrictions.
- Audit record preservation.
- Website and application availability.

The hosting arrangement should be reviewed as the platform grows.

If the shared hosting environment cannot safely support sensitive operations or required application functions, the Committee should consider an appropriate upgrade rather than compromise security.

### 23.1 SSL and Website Security

The platform shall use HTTPS through a valid SSL/TLS certificate.

Certificate installation, renewal and monitoring shall form part of the hosting administration responsibilities.

### 23.2 Hosting and Domain Continuity

The Committee shall maintain administrative control over the hosting account, domain registration and production environment.

Appropriate recovery access should be established to ensure that the portal remains accessible if a developer or administrator becomes unavailable.

# PART FOURTEEN: MAINTENANCE AND SUSTAINABILITY

## 24. Maintenance and Development Support

The portal will require continuous technical maintenance after deployment.

Maintenance shall be treated as a distinct operational responsibility, separate from annual domain, hosting and SSL costs.

The Committee and appointed developers shall agree on an appropriate maintenance arrangement.

The maintenance arrangement should cover:

- Routine system maintenance.
- Security updates.
- Bug correction.
- Backup monitoring.
- Performance monitoring.
- Technical support.
- Database maintenance.
- Recovery assistance.
- Compatibility updates.
- Approved minor improvements.
- Technical documentation.
- Deployment of approved changes.

### 24.1 Maintenance Cost

The Committee and developers shall agree on the maintenance cost and applicable service arrangements.

The maintenance agreement should clearly distinguish:

1. Annual domain registration and renewal.
2. Annual hosting charges.
3. SSL and related infrastructure costs.
4. Agreed technical maintenance charges.
5. Additional development or feature-expansion costs.

No maintenance amount is fixed by this blueprint.

### 24.2 Change Management

New features and major system modifications should be documented and approved through the appropriate project governance process.

The development team should not independently introduce significant operational changes that affect membership administration, financial management or organisational authority.

# PART TWENTY: GOVERNING PRINCIPLES AND CONCLUSION

## 33. Fundamental Principles of the Portal

The development and operation of the IEDPU portal shall be guided by the following principles:

**1. Organisational Ownership:** The platform and its information shall serve IEDPU and remain under its authorised control.

**2. Administrative Flexibility:** The Initiative Committee shall be able to assign predefined portal roles without making those roles dependent on official IEDPU titles.

**3. Accountability:** Important administrative and financial actions shall be recorded and traceable.

**4. Financial Transparency:** Members shall have access to comprehensive and reliable records of their financial transactions.

**5. Branch Participation:** Branches shall remain central to membership verification and grassroots administration.

**6. Data Protection:** Personal information shall be protected against unauthorised access and disclosure.

**7. Expandability:** The platform shall accommodate new organisational requirements without undermining existing functions.

**8. Constitutional Alignment:** The portal shall support, not replace or contradict, the Constitution and approved governance arrangements of IEDPU.

**9. Sustainability:** Hosting, maintenance, technical support and administrative continuity shall be planned as ongoing responsibilities.

**10. Practicality:** Development shall prioritise functional services that address real organisational needs.

## 34. Conclusion

The proposed IEDPU North Zone Digital Membership, Administration and Development Portal represents an important step towards strengthening the organisation's institutional capacity.

It is intended to move membership management from fragmented records towards a unified digital register, replace informal financial tracking with transparent records, strengthen branch administration and provide reliable channels for official communication.

More importantly, it will give IEDPU a clearer understanding of its membership strength and organisational reach.

The project is not intended to be a simple website or registration form. It is a long-term institutional platform designed to support the administration, accountability and development of the Union.

Its success will depend on the quality of its implementation, the participation of branches, the accuracy of its information, the protection of members' records and the commitment of the organisation to using it consistently.

The immediate priority is to establish the foundation, implement the core membership functions and progressively introduce the additional services described in this blueprint.

**The guiding objective is to know our membership, organise our strength, improve our administration and build a stronger foundation for the future of IEDPU.**
